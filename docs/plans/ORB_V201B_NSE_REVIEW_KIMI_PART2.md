## D. Flags on the current system

**1. One trade per day — flag: structurally flawed, partially.**
On chop days one-and-done is optimal; on trend days your first shakeout is often the *setup* and the second entry is the best trade. A vetoed signal should never consume the day (your VETO-01 fix — correct). Also: a signal at 11:25 consuming the daily slot with a 2R target and 15:10 stop is a timing flaw — **entries after 10:45 need TIGHTEN_TARGET applied** because achievable R shrinks with time-to-close. Fix via ranked addition #7 + target truncation.

**2. 11:30 cutoff — flag: defensible but leaves the strongest window.**
You discard 13:30–14:45 — the post-Europe-open and expiry-afternoon acceleration window. Two acceptable answers: (a) keep morning-only, acknowledging it as a deliberate scope cut; (b) add an afternoon module with **its own levels** (day's high/low, PDH/PDL — *not* the morning OR), entries 13:45–14:15, hard flat 15:00. What you cannot do is extend morning-OR levels into the afternoon — level staleness after ~90 min is a real degradation.

**3. Range-VWAP vs session-VWAP — flag: your planner instinct is right.**
Range-VWAP (first 15 min) correlates ~1 with price — vacuous confirmation. Replace with session VWAP from 09:15. Caveat: pre-10:00 the session VWAP is dominated by opening prints, so for 09:30–10:00 signals use ORM/opening-drive logic; after 10:00 require a close on the VWAP side. On gap days add a PDC-anchored VWAP — price between the two VWAPs = contested zone → HALF_SIZE.

**4. Stop inconsistency — flag: backtest/live E3-class mismatch.**
System doc says breakout stop = opposite OR side; memo examples use ORM. Opposite-side on wide OR → absurd R target, positions undersized, backtest R-multiples inflated; ORM stop → whipsaw doubles on chop. **Deterministic rule: stop = opposite OR side if ORW ≤ 0.6×ATR; else ORM; else skip.** Must be identical in backtester and live path.

**5. Event calendar — flag: highest value/effort miss.**
RBI MPC, results, Budget — plain date list kills the worst whipsaws. MPC announcement at ~10:00 sits inside the entry window.

**6. Gap definitions — flag: two definitions (touch vs 5m close) acceptable, but must be synchronized backtest↔live.** Missing ex-dividend adjustment (see #4 above).

**7. First-bar contamination — flag:** the 09:15–09:20 bar includes the pre-open match print. Guard: first-bar volume z-score/range check; else shift OR base to 09:20–09:35.

**8. Circuit/ASM/T2T exclusion — flag: catastrophic-tail risk.** A locked-circuit stock on an intraday short becomes an auction/delivery-penalty event. Structural exclusion, not a gate.

**9. Time stop 15:10 — endorse.** Pre-closing VWAP games + broker MIS auto-square-off ~15:15–15:20. Keep.

**10. PIT wiring — flag:** ATR(14) from D-1 close is correct; ensure the live pipeline carries the D-1 snapshot (your S9). Anything sourced from NSE's public web chain is **15-min delayed** — either rewire via broker API or demote to D-1-only gates.
